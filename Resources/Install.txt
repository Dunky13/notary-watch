Install Notary Watch

1. Drag Notary Watch.app onto the Applications shortcut.
2. Eject this disk image.
3. Open Notary Watch from Applications.
4. Follow the setup guide to connect your Apple notarization profiles.

If updating, quit Notary Watch before copying and choose Replace in Finder.

Requires macOS 14 or later and Xcode's notarytool for Apple account access.
The app supports both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs in universal builds.

Development downloads use a local ad hoc signature. Public signed downloads
are produced separately with Developer ID signing and Apple notarization.

Removing a saved profile from Notary Watch leaves its credentials in Keychain.
